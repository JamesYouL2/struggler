Generated by `scripts/queue_coup_discount.sh`. The maintainer's reading is that
the variable does not make sense: a coup is already priced over its dice and
their board consequences, so a 0.9 on top is a placement preference and not a
rule. The 2026-09-12 arm read 0.526 [0.452, 0.600] but stopped early at 77 of
256 seeds. The experiment below plays every seed at 1.0 on the shipped code; the
gate plays branch `delete/coup-discount`, which deletes the variable, against `07d553a`.

## What this does not say

ACCEPTED means not measurably worse, never better. Whether to merge the branch
is the maintainer's call; this script dispatched the gate regardless of the
experiment's result. Nobody has read these numbers yet.
