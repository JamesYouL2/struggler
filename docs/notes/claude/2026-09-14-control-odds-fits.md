The maintainer's formula, 2026-09-13: value of scoring x flip ability ** turns x P(scoring) x (a / Ops for you to control + b / Ops for them to control), with the constants to fit, the division in doubt, and overprotection moving the odds. Flip ability and the two Ops counts both describe whether the country is ours when it scores, so this measures that probability and fits shapes to it: a (controller, stability) table, reciprocal (1/(1+c)), contest share (log-cost difference), exponential (exp(-c/lambda)) and linear, each with and without overprotection. Fitted on even seeds, scored on odd seeds; battlegrounds only.
