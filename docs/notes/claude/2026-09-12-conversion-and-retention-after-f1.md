## What this does not say

Not a strength measurement. The bot that played these games is the
shipped one, whose access pricing came from the biased figures, so this
measures that bot's games and not an unconditioned conversion rate. The
audit also notes opportunities within a game are correlated, so the
binomial intervals understate the uncertainty. Horizon 2 is censored
hardest in games that end early. Nobody has read these numbers yet.
