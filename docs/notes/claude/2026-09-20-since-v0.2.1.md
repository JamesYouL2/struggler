# Since v0.2.1: 109 commits, and the bot is level with where it started

2026-09-20. Written because the question is worth answering precisely, and
because the precise answer is not the obvious one.

## First, the premise needs correcting

"Nothing improved" is not quite what happened, and the difference matters.

| | HEAD, 2026-09-18 (128 seeds) | HEAD, 2026-09-20 (1024 seeds) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| vs v0.2.0 | 0.402 | **0.501** [0.485, 0.517] |
| vs v0.2.1 | 0.426 | **0.505** [0.489, 0.521] |
| vs `07d553a` (#100) | 0.400 | **0.534** [0.518, 0.550] |
| vs `bc5ef93` | 0.483 | 0.502 [0.485, 0.519] |
| vs v0.1.0 | 0.504 | 0.576 [0.559, 0.593] |

The bot gained about **0.10 against every anchor in two days**. That is
real, it is the largest movement in the project's recorded history, and it
is why the drift canary passes.

What did *not* happen is passing anything. Against v0.2.0, v0.2.1,
`bc5ef93` and v0.3.4 the bot now reads 0.501-0.508: **level**. So the
honest statement is not "nothing improved" but:

> **109 commits after v0.2.1, the bot plays v0.2.1 to a draw.** The period
> contains a large regression and its repair, and nets to zero.

## Where the period went

Of 109 non-merge commits, 37 are `docs` and 9 `notes`; 13 are `feat`, 11
`fix`, 4 `perf`. The strength-moving work, with its measured effect:

| what | measured | against |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Hand safety, audit F1-F5 + last-window guard | **+0.043** [+0.032, +0.055] | old main, paired, 1024 seeds |
| Region margin deleted | **+0.031** | main, paired, 1024 seeds |
| `vp_swing` restored to 3.0 | **+0.039** [+0.019, +0.059] | main, paired, 1024 seeds |
| `access_chain` dropped | 0 by construction (it was already 0) | -- |
| Fitted country weights | +0.018 vs tiers, **-0.031 vs `07d553a`** | reverted |
| Sandbox search budget | -0.001 [-0.002, 0.000] | free, not a gain |
| Three exact ranking speedups | 0 by construction | behaviour-identical |
| VP potential in the ranking | +0.021 [-0.009, +0.052] | inconclusive, ships off |

The three real gains sum to +0.113, which is close to the +0.10 the anchor
ladder moved. The arithmetic is consistent. **The question is why +0.113 of
gain leaves the bot level with a bot from before any of it.**

## The answer: two of the three were repairs

- **`vp_swing` 3.0 is a restoration, not a discovery.** Commit `52bb329`
  flattened the VP turn curve *from* 2.0, and the
  [bisect](2026-09-19-bisect-v0.2.1.md) found that flattening was the drift.
  Setting it to 3.0 recovers what `52bb329` removed and a little more.
- **The region margin deletion removes a term added in the same period.**
  It was fitted, landed, and measured inert; deleting it is the period
  undoing its own work.
- **The hand safety is the one that is not a repair of this period's
  damage** -- the audit's F1-F5 were long-standing defects. It is also the
  largest single gain, and it took the USSR seat's nuclear losses from 169
  of 512 to 37.

So roughly: **one genuine advance, two undos, and a lot of infrastructure.**
Net level is exactly what that adds up to.

## Why the individually-positive features do not sum

This is the part worth keeping, because it will recur.

Every feature added between v0.2.1 and `07d553a` reads as a *gain* when
ablated: the reply look-ahead, deck-tracking rival urgency, Coup replies,
the final-scoring bucket, `access`. The
[drift note](2026-09-18-drift-located-at-v0.2.1-v0.2.3.md) records that
"every ablated feature is a gain". And yet the bot carrying all of them is
level with the bot carrying none of them.

Three explanations, and what each has behind it:

1. **The ablations were measured against a weakened opponent.** They ran
   against `07d553a`, which sits *inside* the regression. Beating a bot with
   a flattened VP curve may measure exploitation of that flaw rather than
   strength. Support: HEAD reads 0.534 against `07d553a` and 0.502 against
   `bc5ef93`, which is the commit *before* `52bb329`. The same bot, two
   anchors, 0.032 apart -- and the harder anchor is the older one.
2. **The features overlap.** Several price the same thing by different
   routes (`access` and its chain; the region margin and the region term;
   the potential and `region_potential`). Gains measured one-at-a-time are
   not additive when the terms are substitutes. Support: removing the region
   term from under the fitted weights costs 0.067, far more than the fitted
   weights are worth -- the layers are not independent.
3. **Strength here is not a total order.** Support: this is *measured*, not
   speculated. The fitted country weights beat the tier bot head to head
   (+0.018, 1024 seeds) and lose to `07d553a` (-0.031, paired, 1024 seeds).
   A change that beats its parent and loses to its grandparent is
   intransitivity, and it means "better than what we shipped last" is not a
   proxy for "stronger".

(1) and (3) are the same warning from two directions, and together they
say the gate's design -- compare against the immediate parent -- cannot
detect this class of loss. It did not: PR #6 merged on a parent-only gate
and the anchored arm that reverted it ran *after* the merge.

## The seat reading, done properly

The raw seat numbers mislead, and it is worth writing down why.

HEAD against `07d553a` reads **US 0.588, USSR 0.479**, which looks like a
weak USSR seat. It is not. The italy/austria self-play baseline is **US
0.621, USSR 0.379** (v0.3.1's identity row, 2026-09-18): the opening itself
is worth about 0.24 to the US. Seat scores have to be read against that
baseline, not against 0.500.

| seat | vs `07d553a` | self-play baseline | difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| US | 0.588 | 0.621 | **-0.033** |
| USSR | 0.479 | 0.379 | **+0.100** |

Read correctly, **HEAD's advantage over `07d553a` is entirely the USSR
seat, and the US seat has gone slightly backwards.** That inverts the
2026-09-18 reading ("the loss is mostly on the US seat"), which was taken
before the hand safety landed -- and the hand safety is a USSR-seat fix,
since that seat is the one that nukes itself.

Two consequences:

- **The USSR seat is where the gains have come from, and the US seat is now
  the neglected one.** Every recent measurement looked at the USSR because
  that is where the variance was; the US seat quietly lost 0.033.
- **Never quote a seat score without the baseline.** 0.479 is a good USSR
  result and 0.588 is a mediocre US one, which is the opposite of how they
  read.

## What to do differently, concretely

1. **Anchor before merge, not after.** Already the stated lesson of PR #6;
   it has now cost the project one revert and one two-day bisect.
2. **`bc5ef93`, not `07d553a`, is the opponent that matters.** It is the
   strongest anchor there is and the one HEAD is only level with. An arm
   that beats `07d553a` and is not run against `bc5ef93` has not been
   tested against the best available opponent.
3. **Measure the US seat.** It is down 0.033 against its own baseline and
   nothing in the current queue looks at it.
4. **Expect substitution.** Before adding a term, ask which existing term
   prices the same thing, and ablate the pair, not the addition.

## What this note does not claim

The 2026-09-18 anchor readings are 128 seeds (+/-0.045); the 2026-09-20
ones are 1024 (+/-0.016). The "+0.10 against every anchor" is therefore a
comparison of a noisy reading with a precise one, and its size is uncertain
even though its sign is not.

Nothing here identifies *which* of the three explanations dominates. They
are separable -- (1) by running the ablations against `bc5ef93`, (2) by
pairwise ablation, (3) by the registered `fit-intransitive-*` arms -- and
none of those has been run.
