**A step of 0.050 between two adjacent anchors, and only one commit in the
gap changes play.** `bc5ef93..52bb329` is four commits: two benchmark
fixes, one docs commit, and `52bb329` itself. Every anchor at or before
`bc5ef93` beats main; every anchor from `52bb329` on is level with it or
loses. The five older readings are flat around 0.47 and the four newer ones
are flat around 0.50, so this is a step and not a slope -- the same shape
the 128-seed series suggested and could not locate.

**52bb329 is `feat(bots): ship a flat VP curve and drop the access chain
term`.** Two weights, changed in one commit:

- `vp_swing` 2.0 -> 1.0, flattening the VP curve. Measured inert at the
  time: 0.491 +/-0.026 over 256 seeds, and 0.497 +/-0.072 over 78.
- `access_chain` 0.4 -> 0.0, switching off the second loop in `access()`
  (a battleground two steps away through a country nobody holds yet).
  Measured 0.491 +/-0.063 over **109 seeds**.

`d941a5b` then deleted both weights and their code, correctly calling that
deletion arithmetically inert -- `1.0 ** x` and `0.0 * x` are identities.
The deletion is not the regression; the settings in 52bb329 are.

**What this says about the measurements that justified it.** Both readings
were taken at 78-256 seeds, where a half-width of +/-0.03 to +/-0.07 cannot
see an effect of 0.03-0.05. "Inert" meant "not visible at this sample
size", and the sample size was the thing that made it invisible. This is
the same failure as PR #6's parent-only gate, one axis over: there the
comparison was against the wrong opponent, here it was at the wrong power.

**Caveats.** Only `src/struggler/bots` is snapshotted, so engine and data
changes in the range are not measured. Adjacent anchors are different
opponents on the same seeds, so the 0.050 is a difference of two readings
(+/-0.017 each), not a paired difference. Four shards stalled on one game
each and reported their other 127 seeds.
