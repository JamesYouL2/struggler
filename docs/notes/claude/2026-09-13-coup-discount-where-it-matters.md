## What this does not say

ACCEPTED means not measurably worse, never better; a drift "ok" means no
measurable drift, not equal strength. Scores are the candidate's (HEAD's, or
the arm's): above 0.500 is the candidate doing better. Nobody has read these
numbers yet.
