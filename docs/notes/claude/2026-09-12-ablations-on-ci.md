Why each arm, as dispatched:

- **first-mover-0** -- An additive term on contested battlegrounds only; guess/underdetermined in models/provenance.json. Additive, so zero does not remove a tiebreak family.
- **margin-guesses-0** -- margin_battleground (0.25) and margin_country (0.05) are guess/underdetermined; margin_presence and margin_live are gate-bounded and stay. This is the untested-constants experiment the dropped whole-margin ablation was not (2026-09-12 handoff).

- **region-1** -- guess/underdetermined; the battleground revamp plan puts region at par as step 2. Measured alone here, which the plan warns is a half-change -- this is a measurement, not a proposal to ship it.
- **reply-model-0** -- reply_model 3 is gate-bounded as not measurably worse, never measured as better. Codex's 2026-09-13 audit found three defects in it (Q1 unreachable replies, Q2 flat doubled cost, F5 replies after the last move). This measures what the search, defects included, is worth before anyone fixes it.
- **scoring-hand-1** -- Neutral urgency for a held scoring card, the test docs/STRATEGIC_SIMPLIFICATION.md proposes; guess/underdetermined.

## What this does not say

The score is the ARM's, against the shipped weights: above 0.500 means the
ablated bot did better. ACCEPTED means the ablated weights are not
measurably worse, never that the term is useless -- at these sizes most
real effects are unresolved, and several arms touch few decisions. A
single-weight result is also exactly the half-change the battleground
revamp plan warns against shipping. Nobody has read these numbers yet.
